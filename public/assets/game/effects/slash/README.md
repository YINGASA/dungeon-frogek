# slash

Future weapon slash and thrust effects. Recommended names include `slash_short_sword.png`, `slash_heavy_blade.png`, `thrust_spear.png`, and `slash_dual_daggers.png`.

These effects are visual only. They should help players read weapon identity without changing hitboxes, damage, cooldown, knockback, or double-hit behavior.

Keep contrast high enough to read during combat, but avoid covering damage numbers, enemy telegraphs, or HUD elements.
